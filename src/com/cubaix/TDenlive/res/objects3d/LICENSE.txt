3Denlive library of 3D objects - licences

The 3D models listed in objects.txt are NOT embedded in the application: each one is downloaded
from its source at its first use into ~/3Denlive/Models/Objects3D/ and then read from there.
They keep the licence of their author, given below per pack (CC0 1.0 = public domain dedication,
no attribution required; CC-BY 3.0 = attribution required: credit the author as listed).
